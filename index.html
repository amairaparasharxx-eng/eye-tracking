Eye Movement Observation

Webcam-based, non-diagnostic prototype

Important: This prototype records webcam-derived measurements such as eyelid position, gaze direction, gaze stability and head movement. It does not diagnose myasthenia gravis or any other condition. The requested clinical signs are presented only as observational categories and require qualified clinical assessment.

Before you begin

  • Use a well-lit room and keep your face clearly visible.
  • Keep the camera at approximately eye level.
  • Keep your head reasonably still unless the instruction says otherwise.
  • Remove sunglasses and anything covering the eyes.
  • Stop if you become uncomfortable, dizzy, or develop visual discomfort.
